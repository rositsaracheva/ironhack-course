DATA DICTIONARY

customers.csv
- customer_id: unique customer key
- customer_name: generated customer name
- email: example email address
- city, region: customer geography
- signup_date: customer registration date
- customer_segment: Consumer, Corporate, or Small Business

products.csv
- product_id: unique product key
- product_name: product description
- category: Electronics, Furniture, Stationery, Accessories
- unit_cost: illustrative cost per unit
- list_price: illustrative selling price per unit

orders.csv
- order_id: unique order key
- order_date: order date
- customer_id: customer foreign key
- product_id: product foreign key
- quantity: units ordered
- unit_price: selling price per unit
- discount_pct: discount fraction (e.g. 0.10 = 10%); some values are null
- order_status: Delivered, Shipped, Pending, Cancelled, Returned
- shipping_date: ship date; some values are null
- payment_method: payment channel

NOTE: All records and names are synthetic and intended for learning only.
